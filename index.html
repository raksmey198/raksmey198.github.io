About

Research focus: regional geotechnical site characterization, settlement, and ground improvement.

Research

Physics-informed machine learning for site characterization and spatial transfer learning

  • Physics-informed machine learning for ground settlement prediction in spatially heterogeneous ground conditions, combining physical governing equations, inverse parameter estimation, and field monitoring data.
  • Three-dimensional site characterization and spatial transfer learning for geotechnical prediction across sites with varying ground improvement conditions and limited subsurface investigation.

Journal paper

  • Thea, R., Jeong, Y., Chien, D., and Lee, C. (2026). Spatial encoding strategies for XGBoost-based consolidation settlement prediction: a comparative study at Songdo New City. Journal of the Korean Geotechnical Society, 42(4), 75-89. doi:10.7843/kgs.2026.42.4.75
    Compares five ways of encoding location (raw coordinates, distance to neighboring points, buffer distance, polynomial, radial basis functions) in an XGBoost model. Distance to neighboring points performed best (test R² = 0.776 against 0.719 for raw coordinates).

Conference abstracts
